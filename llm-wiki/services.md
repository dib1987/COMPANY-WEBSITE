# Services: Bharat AI Automation Labs

We offer six services. Each one is scoped to a specific business problem — not a generic "AI solution."

---

## 1. AI Lead Recovery & Follow-up Automation
**What it does:** Captures leads the moment they come in and follows up automatically — so no lead goes cold because someone forgot to call back.

**Best for:** Gyms, fitness studios, dental clinics, healthcare clinics, real estate agents, coaching businesses, and any service business where speed-to-lead matters.

**Typical problems it solves:**
- Missed calls that never get followed up
- Leads that go cold after the first contact
- Manual follow-up that depends on individual staff memory
- Trial/consultation sign-ups that don't convert

---

## 2. AI Business Process Automation
**What it does:** Identifies and automates the repetitive manual steps that slow down operations — data entry, status updates, internal notifications, handoffs between teams.

**Best for:** Operations-heavy businesses where staff spend hours on tasks that could run automatically.

**Typical problems it solves:**
- Manual data transfer between systems
- Approval chains that bottleneck on one person
- Status updates that require human effort to generate

---

## 3. AI Document Processing
**What it does:** Extracts, validates, and routes information from documents — intake forms, contracts, referrals, invoices — without manual review.

**Best for:** Healthcare, insurance, finance, HR departments, legal admin.

**Typical problems it solves:**
- Staff manually reading and re-keying form data
- Documents sitting in inboxes waiting for someone to process them
- Inconsistent data quality from manual entry

---

## 4. AI Training & Enablement *(Strongest area)*
**What it does:** Trains your team to use AI tools effectively in their actual daily work — not a generic "AI 101" course, but practical sessions built around your specific workflows and tools.

**Best for:** Teams adopting AI tools who don't know where to start, founders who want to use AI in their business, startups building AI into their product.

**Typical problems it solves:**
- Team bought AI tools but adoption is low
- Founders spending hours on tasks AI could handle
- No internal knowledge on how to prompt or evaluate AI output

---

## 5. Custom AI Agents & Workflows
**What it does:** Builds purpose-built AI agents that handle multi-step tasks autonomously — research, outreach, qualification, document generation, and more.

**Best for:** Businesses with a specific recurring workflow that needs AI judgment, not just rule-based automation.

**Typical problems it solves:**
- Complex tasks that need context and decision-making, not just triggers
- Workflows that span multiple tools and require AI to bridge them

---

## 6. AI Readiness Consultation
**What it does:** A structured strategy session that maps where your business stands on AI adoption and gives you a prioritized roadmap — what to automate first, what tools to use, what to avoid.

**Best for:** Business owners and founders who want to understand AI's role in their business before committing to implementation.

**Typical outcome:** A clear, prioritized list of automation opportunities ranked by effort and impact — so you know exactly where to start.
