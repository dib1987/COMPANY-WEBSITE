# FAQ: Bharat AI Automation Labs

Common questions from real visitors. Answers are direct — no fluff.

---

**Q: What does Bharat AI Automation Labs do?**

We help small and mid-size businesses automate the manual, repetitive work that's slowing them down or costing them leads. Things like: following up with enquiries automatically, processing documents without manual data entry, training your team to use AI tools properly. We map your actual workflow, find where automation pays off, and build it.

---

**Q: Do you work with small businesses?**

Yes. Most of our clients are small businesses — gyms, dental clinics, real estate agencies, coaching businesses, startups. You don't need a big IT team or a large budget to benefit from automation. We start with whatever resources you have.

---

**Q: How long does it take to build a system?**

Depends on complexity. A focused automation (e.g. lead follow-up for one channel) can go from brief to live in a few days. A full multi-step AI agent with integrations typically takes 2–6 weeks. We tell you the timeline upfront before any work starts.

---

**Q: Do I need technical knowledge to use your systems?**

No. We build for non-technical users. The systems we build run in the background — you see the results (booked appointments, followed-up leads, processed documents), not the machinery. We also train your team so they know how to work alongside the automation.

---

**Q: What tools do you integrate with?**

We work with the tools you already use. Common ones: Google Workspace, HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Slack, WhatsApp Business, Calendly, Zapier, Make.com, GoHighLevel, various CRMs, and most cloud-based practice management software. If you're using something specific, ask — we'll tell you honestly if we can connect it.

---

**Q: What industries do you serve?**

Our strongest areas: gyms and fitness studios, dental and healthcare clinics, real estate, coaching and consulting, startups, and professional services. We've also worked with insurance, finance, HR, and legal admin. If your business has repetitive manual work or a lead follow-up problem, we can almost certainly help.

---

**Q: How much does it cost?**

It depends on what you're building. Rough ranges:
- Single automation: under £1,000
- Full lead recovery or document pipeline: £1,000–£5,000
- Multi-step AI agent: £5,000–£15,000
- Enterprise-level custom workflow: £15,000+

Everything starts with a free audit — so you know what you actually need before committing to anything.

---

**Q: What is a free AI automation audit?**

It's a no-obligation review of your business workflow. You tell us how work moves through your business. We find 3–5 places where automation can save time, recover leads, or cut manual effort — ranked by impact. You get those findings back within one business day. No charge, no pitch.

---

**Q: Can AI replace my staff?**

That's not what we build. We build automation that handles the repetitive, rules-based parts of a job — so your staff can focus on the work that actually needs a human. The businesses we work with use automation to make their team more effective, not smaller.
