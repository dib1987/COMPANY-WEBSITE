# Use Cases: Bharat AI Automation Labs

Four real examples of how we've applied AI automation. These are the kinds of results our work produces.

---

## Use Case 1: Gym & Fitness Studio — Lead-to-Member Conversion

**Industry:** Gym / Fitness studio  
**Problem:** Leads were coming in through the website and Facebook ads, but staff were following up manually — sometimes 2-3 days later. By then, the prospect had joined a competitor or simply lost interest.  
**Solution:** Built an AI lead recovery workflow that triggers within minutes of a new lead coming in. The system sends a personalised message, qualifies interest, and books a trial session automatically — without any staff involvement.  
**Outcome:** No lead goes uncontacted. Follow-up happens at the moment of peak interest.  
**Key result:** Significant increase in trial-to-member conversion rate; staff freed from chasing cold leads.

---

## Use Case 2: Dental Clinic — Recovering Missed Appointment Revenue

**Industry:** Dental / Healthcare clinic  
**Problem:** Patients were cancelling appointments with less than 24 hours notice. The slot couldn't be filled in time. That's direct lost revenue — often £150–£400 per missed appointment.  
**Solution:** Built an automated recall and rebooking system. When a cancellation comes in, the system immediately contacts patients on a waiting list and fills the slot automatically.  
**Outcome:** Slots get filled instead of sitting empty. The receptionist is notified when a new booking is confirmed.  
**Key result:** Meaningful reduction in lost appointment revenue per month; zero additional work from the front desk.

## Use Case 3: Healthcare / Admin — Cutting Manual Document Review

**Industry:** Healthcare / Insurance / Finance  
**Problem:** Staff were spending hours reading incoming referral forms, intake documents, and insurance paperwork — then manually entering data into the system.  
**Solution:** Built a document processing pipeline using AI to extract key fields, validate them against business rules, and route documents to the right queue automatically. Exceptions flagged for human review.  
**Outcome:** Near-zero manual data entry for standard documents. Staff focus only on exceptions.  
**Key result:** Hours saved per week per staff member; fewer data entry errors.

## Use Case 4: Teams & Founders — AI Training & Enablement

**Industry:** Startups / Coaching / Professional Services  
**Problem:** Founders and teams had access to AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Notion AI, etc.) but weren't using them effectively. Either low adoption, or using them for trivial tasks while missing the high-impact ones.  
**Solution:** Delivered practical AI training sessions built around the team's actual workflows — not generic AI theory. Sessions covered: prompt engineering for their specific use cases, building repeatable AI workflows, evaluating AI output quality.  
**Outcome:** Team using AI tools daily for real tasks — research, drafting, analysis, client communication.  
**Key result:** Measurable reduction in time spent on repeatable cognitive tasks; founders reclaiming hours per week.
