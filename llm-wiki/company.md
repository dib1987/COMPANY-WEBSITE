# Company: Bharat AI Automation Labs

## Who We Are
Bharat AI Automation Labs is a founder-led AI automation consultancy. We help small and mid-size businesses stop losing leads, cut manual work, and run more efficiently — using AI and automation built on the tools they already have.

We are not a software agency. We don't sell off-the-shelf software. We map your actual workflow, find where work breaks down, and build targeted automation that solves the right problem.

## Core Philosophy
**Process-first, not hype.** We start with how work actually moves through your business — not with what AI can theoretically do.

Three working principles:
1. **Start with the process** — understand the bottleneck before proposing a solution
2. **Build for your stack** — use tools you already have where possible; only add what's necessary
3. **Measure the outcome** — define success before building, track it after
