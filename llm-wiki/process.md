# Engagement Process: Bharat AI Automation Labs

## How Working With Us Works

Everything starts with a free audit — no commitment, no sales pitch.

---

## Step 1: Free AI Automation Opportunity Audit

You share how your business currently operates — where work comes in, how it moves, where it gets stuck. This can be done over email or a short call.

We look at:
- Where leads, documents, or tasks are falling through the cracks
- Where staff are doing repetitive manual work that AI could handle
- Where delays are costing you revenue or customer experience

**No obligation. No charge. No pitch deck.**

---

## Step 2: We Map It and Find 3–5 Opportunities

Our team reviews your workflow and identifies the highest-impact automation opportunities — ranked by effort and return.

We look specifically for:
- Quick wins (automations that take days to build and pay back immediately)
- Structural fixes (longer builds that address a root-cause bottleneck)
- Risks to avoid (things that sound like good AI use cases but aren't)

---

## Step 3: You Get the Findings — Within One Business Day

You receive a prioritised list of 3–5 automation opportunities, ranked by impact. Written in plain language — no jargon.

You can take this list and do nothing, implement it yourself, or work with us to build it. Your choice.

---

## Step 4: 30-Minute Call (If You Want to Go Deeper)

If you want to go deeper on one or more opportunities, we set up a 30-minute call to walk through your specific bottlenecks in detail and discuss what building would look like.

---

## Budget Ranges

We work across a range of project sizes. Rough budget brackets:

| Budget | What it typically covers |
|---|---|
| Under £1,000 | A single automation (e.g. lead follow-up workflow for one channel) |
| £1,000–£5,000 | A full lead recovery system or document processing pipeline |
| £5,000–£15,000 | Multi-step AI agent or end-to-end process automation |
| £15,000+ | Enterprise-level custom AI workflow with integrations and training |

We don't require a minimum engagement size. If your problem is small and well-defined, we'll tell you honestly what it costs.

---

Simply reach out via the contact form, or tell BharatAi (our website assistant) that you're interested — and we'll kick off the process from there.
